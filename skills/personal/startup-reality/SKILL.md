# Startup Reality

- Perfect architecture is not always the goal
- Delivery speed matters
- Prefer incremental improvement
- Avoid premature optimization
- Solve the current business problem first
- Technical debt is acceptable when intentional and visible, subject to scope-discipline and repository constraints.
- Obtain human approval before accepting material new technical debt or weakening agreed quality criteria. Explain the delivery benefit, consequences, and follow-up; do not silently trade away correctness, security, or required verification.
